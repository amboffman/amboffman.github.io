I'm a Senior Fullstack Engineer with 6+ years building and scaling data-driven web applications across PHP and the modern JavaScript ecosystem. At Qualtrics I own projects end to end — from backend services and REST/GraphQL APIs to the React frontends they power — on a SaaS platform serving enterprise brands at high traffic.

My specialty is the unglamorous, high-stakes work: migrating legacy systems to modern, maintainable architectures without breaking what's already in production. Incremental re-architecture, containerized environments, standardized CI/CD — reliability as a feature.

I treat AI as a force multiplier across the whole lifecycle — code generation, review, test scaffolding, refactoring — while keeping human judgment on architecture and product. This site is the proof: hand-built, zero frameworks, shipped with AI in the loop.

The rest of the job is people: mentoring engineers through code review and pair programming, and translating requirements into software that ships.
